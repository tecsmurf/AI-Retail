# Pitch

## One-Liner
PurpleSol turns your existing CCTV cameras into a Google Analytics dashboard for your physical retail store.

## Problem (30 seconds)
Retailers spend millions on CCTV but only use it for security. Meanwhile, they have zero visibility into what happens between a customer walking in and making a purchase. They can't answer: which zones attract attention? Which layouts drive sales? Where are customers dropping off?

## Solution (30 seconds)
PurpleSol processes your existing CCTV footage using AI (YOLO11 + ByteTrack) to detect, track, and analyze customer behavior in real-time. It generates business events — zone visits, dwell times, journey paths — and presents them in a beautiful analytics dashboard.

## Demo Highlights (60 seconds)
1. **Dashboard** — 247 visitors today, 23.4% conversion, ₹48,750 revenue
2. **Hourly Footfall** — Peak at 6 PM, traffic patterns visible
3. **Conversion Funnel** — See exactly where customers drop off
4. **Zone Rankings** — Faces Canada is #1 most visited, Makeup Unit has highest dwell time
5. **Customer Journeys** — Top 5 paths through the store, conversion vs browse paths
6. **Anomaly Alerts** — Long queue detected at billing, automatic alert generated
7. **Multi-Store** — Store 1 vs Store 2 comparison

## Business Value
- **Increase conversion** by optimizing store layout based on traffic data
- **Reduce queue abandonment** with early alert system
- **Boost revenue per visitor** by placing high-margin products in high-traffic zones
- **Cut analysis time** from weeks of manual observation to real-time dashboards

## Tech Stack
YOLO11 → ByteTrack → FastAPI → PostgreSQL → Next.js

## Ask
Deploy across all Purplle retail stores to validate ROI within 90 days.
