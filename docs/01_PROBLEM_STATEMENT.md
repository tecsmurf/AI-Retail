# Problem Statement

## The Retail Visibility Gap

Physical retail stores generate billions in revenue globally, yet most retailers operate with a significant blind spot: **what happens between a customer entering the store and making a purchase.**

### What Retailers Know
- POS transaction data (what was bought, when, how much)
- Inventory levels
- Staff schedules

### What Retailers Don't Know
- How many people entered vs. how many purchased (true conversion)
- Which zones attract the most foot traffic
- How long customers spend in different areas (dwell time)
- Which customer journeys lead to purchases
- Why customers leave without buying (abandonment patterns)
- When queue congestion drives customers away
- Which store layouts perform better

### Business Impact
- **Lost revenue** from poorly optimized store layouts
- **Understaffing** during peak hours
- **Overstocked shelves** in low-traffic zones
- **Customer churn** from long queue wait times
- **Missed cross-selling** opportunities between zones

### Current Solutions and Their Limitations
| Solution | Limitation |
|----------|-----------|
| Manual counting | Inaccurate, no zone-level data |
| WiFi/Bluetooth tracking | Requires customer opt-in, privacy concerns |
| Infrared counters | Entry/exit only, no zone intelligence |
| Traditional BI tools | No spatial/behavioral data |

### PurpleSol's Approach
Leverage **existing CCTV infrastructure** (no new hardware) with AI-powered computer vision to generate the missing behavioral intelligence layer.
