# PurpleSol — Project Overview

## Vision
PurpleSol is an AI Retail Intelligence Platform that transforms CCTV footage into real-time retail analytics and customer journey intelligence. Think "Google Analytics for Physical Retail Stores."

## Problem
Retailers know what customers purchase but lack visibility into pre-purchase behavior:
- Which zones attract the most attention?
- Where do customers spend the most time?
- Which customer journeys lead to purchases?
- What is the relationship between store behavior and sales?

## Solution
An end-to-end system that processes CCTV footage through computer vision (YOLO11 + ByteTrack), generates business events, reconstructs customer journeys, and presents actionable insights through a modern SaaS-quality dashboard.

## Target Users
- Retail store managers
- Operations teams
- Business analysts
- Retail chain executives

## Key Differentiators
1. **Event-driven architecture** — Not just detections, but business events
2. **Journey reconstruction** — Full customer path from entry to exit
3. **POS correlation** — Link behavior to actual purchases
4. **Multi-store support** — Cross-store comparison and benchmarking
5. **Real-time monitoring** — WebSocket-powered live updates
6. **Anomaly detection** — Automated operational alerts
