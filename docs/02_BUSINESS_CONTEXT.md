# Business Context

## Industry: Beauty & Personal Care Retail

PurpleSol is built for **Purplle**, one of India's leading beauty and personal care platforms, operating physical retail stores across Mumbai.

## Stores

### Store 1 — Mumbai
- **Cameras**: 4 (2 zone, 1 entrance, 1 billing)
- **Zones**: 15 brand sections including Salm, TFS, Minimalist, Aqualogica, Foxtale, Juicy Chemistry, Faces Canada, Mars/NY Bae, Men's, L'Oreal, Beauty
- **Features**: Makeup units, fragrance section, cash counter

### Store 2 — Mumbai
- **Cameras**: 4 (2 entrance, 1 billing, 1 zone)
- **Zones**: 7 (Entrance, Left Wall, Right Wall, Center Display 1/2, Makeup Area, Billing)
- **Features**: Wall units, gondolas, makeup stations

## Business KPIs
1. **Footfall** — Daily/hourly visitor counts
2. **Conversion Rate** — Visitors who make a purchase
3. **Dwell Time** — Time spent in each zone
4. **Revenue per Visitor** — Average revenue generated per store visitor
5. **Queue Abandonment** — Customers who leave the queue without purchasing
6. **Zone Effectiveness** — Traffic vs. revenue correlation per zone

## Revenue Model Potential
- Per-store SaaS subscription
- Enterprise license for multi-store chains
- Analytics API access
- Custom reporting and consulting
