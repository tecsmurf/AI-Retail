## 1. What we do

In [all / the listed] stores, we use our existing CCTV cameras to understand
how the store is used:

* how many people come in and leave, by hour and day;
* which areas of the store people walk through and how long they stay there;
* how long billing queues are and how long people wait;
* how these numbers compare with our sales totals for the same hours.

We use this to decide how many billing counters to open, where to place
products, and how to plan staff.

## 2. What we do not do

* We do not use face recognition, and we do not try to identify anyone.
* We do not estimate age, gender or any other personal characteristic.
* We do not link what the cameras see to your name, phone number, loyalty
  account, card or bill.
* We do not follow a person from one camera to another or from one visit to
  the next.

## 3. How the video is handled

**[If the store uses the in-store box]** Video is analysed by a small
computer inside the store, frame by frame, as it arrives. The video used for
counting is not saved by this system and is not sent outside the store. What
leaves the store is a list of events such as "a person entered at 18:02" or
"a person stood in the billing queue for 2 minutes", each tagged with a
temporary number that has no link to who the person is.

**[If recordings are uploaded]** Selected CCTV recordings are uploaded to our
analytics provider's secure storage, analysed automatically, and deleted
within [30] days of upload. The anonymous counts are kept.

From time to time a store manager may save a single still image of a camera
view to mark out store areas on it. We take these when the store is empty
where possible, and delete any that show a visitor.

This analytics does not change how long our ordinary security CCTV
recordings are kept, which is covered by [link to the chain's CCTV policy].

## 4. Who processes it

We use **Footrail** ([operator legal name, address]) to run the analytics
under a contract that lets it use the data only on our instructions and

## 5. How long we keep it

* Uploaded recordings: deleted within [30] days.
* Still images of a camera view: until replaced or removed.
* Anonymous counts and movement data: [for as long as we use the service /
  N years].

## 6. Your rights and questions

You can ask us about this processing, ask for a still image or recording that
shows you to be deleted, or raise a complaint with our grievance officer:

[Name or title], [email], [phone], [address]

We will reply within [period]. Because the counts carry no information that
identifies you, we usually cannot find "your" records in them. If a still
image or recording that shows you is still held, we will delete it.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data
Protection Board of India.
